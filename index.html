Our story

A Brainerd brand, inspired by the woods outside the door

Cozy North started in Brainerd, Minnesota with a simple idea: a Minnesota candle should smell like the place you actually want to be. Up here that means balsam after a snowfall, a warm stove in the cabin, vanilla and birch on a quiet night in, and a breeze coming off the lake.

Our First Drop keeps it small and simple on purpose: five scents, one good size, and every Minnesota candle made to order for Cozy North by our US production partner and shipped direct to you — so what shows up at your door was made for your order, not pulled off a dusty shelf. Good to know

Candle care, shipping & returns

🕯️ Candle care

  • First burn: let the wax melt all the way to the edges (about 2–3 hours) so it doesn't tunnel.
  • Trim the wick to about ¼ inch before each burn.
  • Burn for no more than 4 hours at a time, on a heat-safe surface, away from kids, pets, and drafts.
  • Stop burning when about ½ inch of wax remains.

📦 Shipping & returns

Shipping: Flat $8 per order, US addresses only — no matter if it's 1, 2, or 5 candles. Made-to-order candles need a few business days for pouring and curing before they ship; you'll get tracking by email.
